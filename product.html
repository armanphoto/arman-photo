Food, beverage and product photography for restaurants, bars and brands.

Restaurants, cafés and brands

I photograph dishes, desserts, cocktails and drinks for restaurants, cafés and bars — for menus, delivery apps, websites, social media and ads. Each shoot is planned around where the photos will run, so you get the formats you actually need.

Typical shoots are a new menu, a seasonal set menu, an opening or a product launch. You decide how many dishes or products we photograph, and I can shoot at your venue or in the studio.

For product and jewellery brands I shoot packshots and styled still lifes: rings, earrings and bracelets set against colour, fruit and shadow, wine and spirits, packaging and gift sets.
